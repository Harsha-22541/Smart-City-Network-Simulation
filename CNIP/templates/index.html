Smart City Network Simulation and Congestion Analysis

Academic Laboratory Report
1. Problem Statement

A smart city contains traffic signals, CCTV systems, pollution sensors, smart street lights, a central traffic-management center, and emergency services. These devices continuously generate network traffic and exchange information with control systems. When many devices transmit simultaneously, congestion, buffer overflow, packet drops, and reduced throughput occur.

2. The Leaky Bucket Solution

The Leaky Bucket algorithm controls incoming traffic and reduces buffer overflow. Incoming bursty traffic (specifically CCTV video) is buffered and released at a constant leak rate matching channel capacity. This smooths out packet influx and eliminates tail-drop collapses at the central bottleneck gateway router.

3. Key Experimental Verification
  • Baseline Packet Delivery Ratio (PDR): 32.94% (67.06% packet drops).
  • Leaky Bucket Packet Delivery Ratio (PDR): 83.37% (16.63% packet drops).
  • Emergency Vehicle Delivery: Rescued from 0% delivery to 100% complete delivery.
  • Average Network Goodput: Increased by 78.7% (from 1.46 Mbps to 2.61 Mbps).
